Support

Help with Whendue. Can't find your answer? Email whenduesupport@gmail.com.

Moving to a new iPhone or iPad

Sign in to the same Apple Account, make sure iCloud is on, and install Whendue. Your homes, cars, tasks and history appear on their own after a few moments. Receipt photos stay on the device where they were taken.

Saving or restoring a backup

Tap the cloud icon at the top of the app. Save a backup makes one file you can keep in Files or send to yourself. Restore from a backup loads it again. Export history makes a spreadsheet (CSV).

Reminders aren't showing up

Open Settings > Notifications > Whendue and make sure notifications are allowed. Reminders arrive at 9:00 AM: three days before a task is due, on the day, and weekly once it is overdue.

Mileage

On the Car tab, tap the mileage card to update your odometer. A task that repeats by miles shows how many miles are left. Once a month, Whendue reminds you to update it.

Whendue Pro

Pro adds more than one home or car and service record PDFs. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On a new phone, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.
