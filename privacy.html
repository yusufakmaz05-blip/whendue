Privacy Policy

Whendue · Effective October 27, 2026

In short: Whendue has no accounts. Your tasks, costs, notes and photos stay on your iPhone, and an optional copy lives in your own iCloud. We do not collect your personal information, show ads, or track you, and we never sell data.

What Whendue stores, and where

  • Your tasks and history: the homes and cars you add, tasks, due dates, mileage, costs, notes and your optional monthly budget. They are stored on your device.
  • Receipt photos: if you attach a photo to a finished task, it is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded.
  • iCloud sync: if you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of your tasks, history, homes, cars and budget (not photos) is kept in your own iCloud account so it can follow you to a new iPhone or iPad. This is handled by Apple. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us.
  • Reminders: notifications are created on your device. We do not run a server that sends them.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or give your name or email.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data for marketing.

Purchases

Whendue Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and we never see your card details. To tell whether Pro is active, Whendue uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app identifier and your purchase information from Apple. It does not receive your tasks, photos, or any other content from the app.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications: to remind you when a task is due.
  • Camera / Photos: only when you choose to attach a receipt photo. Photos are only read when you pick one.

Exporting a backup, a spreadsheet, a PDF, or a calendar event uses the standard iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you send it. Whendue does not read your calendar.

Your choices

  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device, including receipt photos.
  • You can stop syncing, or manage the synced copy, in Settings > your name > iCloud.
  • You can save a backup of your data at any time from the app and restore it later.

Children

Whendue is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information that identifies you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct, or delete. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law and have a question, write to us and we will answer it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above, and for any significant change we will say so in the app's release notes.
