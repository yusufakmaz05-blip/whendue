Privacy Policy

Whendue · Effective October 27, 2026

In short: Whendue has no accounts. Your tasks, costs, notes and scanned papers stay on your iPhone, and an optional copy lives in your own iCloud. We do not collect your personal information, show ads, or track you, and we never sell data.

What Whendue stores, and where

  • Your tasks and history: the homes and cars you add, tasks, due dates, mileage, costs, notes and your optional monthly budget. They are stored on your device.
  • Your car's details: fuel type, license plate, make, model, year and VIN, if you enter them, and your fuel log (fill-ups, costs and mileage). They are stored on your device.
  • Receipt and document pictures: when you scan a receipt or a paper (such as a registration), Whendue keeps the photo and a cleaned-up scan, and reads the text on it on your iPhone to fill in the store, amount and date. The reading never leaves your device. The pictures are saved on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud Drive.
  • iCloud sync: if you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of your tasks, history, fuel log, document details, homes, cars and budget, and your receipt and document pictures, is kept in your own iCloud account so it can follow you to a new iPhone or iPad and survive a lost phone. This is handled by Apple. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us.
  • VIN lookup: only if you tap Fill in from VIN, the VIN you typed is sent to the free vehicle database of the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) to look up the make, model and year. Nothing else is sent, and it is not sent to us.
  • Recall check: only if you tap Recalls on a car (United States only), the car's make, model and year are sent to the NHTSA recall list. Nothing else is sent, and it is not sent to us.
  • Reminders: notifications are created on your device. We do not run a server that sends them.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or give your name or email.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data for marketing.

Optional gifts

Whendue is free and everything in it is included. If you choose to support it, a small one-time gift is paid through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and we never see your card details. A gift does not unlock anything, and no third-party service is involved.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications: to remind you when a task is due.
  • Camera / Photos: only when you choose to scan a receipt or paper. Photos are only read when you pick one.

Exporting a backup, a spreadsheet, a PDF, or a calendar event uses the standard iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you send it. Whendue does not read your calendar.

Your choices

  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device, including receipt and document pictures kept on it. Pictures in your iCloud Drive can be removed in Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage.
  • You can stop syncing, or manage the synced copy, in Settings > your name > iCloud.
  • You can save a backup of your data at any time from the app and restore it later.

Children

Whendue is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information that identifies you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct, or delete. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law and have a question, write to us and we will answer it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above, and for any significant change we will say so in the app's release notes.
